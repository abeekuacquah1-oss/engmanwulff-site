An Accra studio,
working across
West Africa.

We make documentary films and photographs for organisations doing real work on the ground — the kind of footage that shows a funder, or a community, exactly what happened and why it mattered.

We've filmed across Ghana, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Sierra Leone, and Togo, and we work in both English and French. Most of our clients are NGOs, development organisations, and institutions, so we're used to working carefully around the people and stories we're trusted with.

How a project usually goes

From the first conversation to the final files.

01

Discover

We start by listening. What are you trying to show, and who actually needs to see it?

02

Plan

We scout locations, line up the people we need to film, and sort out consent before anyone picks up a camera.

03

Produce

We shoot on location, usually over a few days, and we take our time with the people we're filming.

04

Deliver

We edit, colour-grade, and hand over files that are ready to use, whatever the format you need.

Ethics & safeguarding

We film with consent and care. Every project follows informed-consent and child-safeguarding practices, protects the dignity and privacy of the people we work with, and handles all footage and data securely, to the standards our NGO and institutional partners require.
